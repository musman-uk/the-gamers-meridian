Sender's Stamp

In cartography, a meridian is a line of longitude stretching from pole to pole, used as a fixed reference to pinpoint where you stand on Earth. Here, it marks the line where virtual worlds meet real geography.

Whenever a game unfolds in an old city, along a rugged coast, or across a distant mountain range, it is easy to wonder where in the real world you would actually be standing. The Gamer's Meridian was built for that exact curiosity, where you can open the Travel Journal, look up beloved titles, and pin those adventures onto your own globe.
