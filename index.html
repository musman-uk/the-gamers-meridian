Global Postcard is an interactive travel record anchoring virtual world settings from Assassin's Creed (1 & 2) and Uncharted (1 & 2) onto real Earth coordinates.

Each card uses a Dual-Tier Location Schema: presenting game-era setting names (e.g., Jerusalem, Holy Land • 1191 AD) on the front postmark while anchoring real-world geographic metadata on the back.
